Kroka Privacy Policy

Kroka does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

The game saves your best score on your own device only. It never leaves the device.

The game has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change goes live.
